# Privacy Policy for Fave Arena PVP

**Effective Date:** 12 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how "Fave Arena PVP" ("the Bot", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use it on Discord.

## 1. Data We Collect
To function correctly, the Bot may collect and store the following information:
- Discord User IDs (to manage permissions and user-specific data)
- Discord Server (Guild) IDs (to manage server-specific configurations)
- Channel IDs and Role IDs (for command execution and moderation features)

The Bot **does not** collect personal real-world information, passwords, or read messages outside of the commands directed at it.

## 2. How We Use Your Data
We use the collected data strictly to:
- Provide the features and commands offered by the Bot.
- Maintain server security and moderation settings.
- Troubleshoot errors and improve the Bot's performance.

## 3. Data Sharing and Security
We **do not** sell, trade, or rent your data to any third parties. All data is kept secure and is only accessible by the Bot's core development team.

## 4. Data Deletion
If you wish to have your data removed, simply kick the Bot from your server. You can also contact the developer directly on Discord for manual data purging.

## 5. Contact
For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Bot owner via Discord.
